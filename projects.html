burpee-switch

Multiple accounts · one browser

A container-style extension for Chrome, Brave and Edge. Keep work, personal and client logins open in the same window — each tab with its own cookies, colour and label. Optional Burp Suite headers and a highlighter JAR make multi-account traffic readable in Proxy history. No accounts, no cloud, no telemetry.
